The situation

The offer isn't the problem. The waiting is.

Contracts get signed, then fall apart before closing. The process works. The offers are fair. The team is good at what it does.

Here is what we believe happens in that gap. A seller signs. Then they go looking for reassurance, because this is the largest financial decision most people ever make and they just made it in a week. They search your name. They ask their sister. They read comments from strangers.

What they find doesn't answer the thing actually keeping them up at night. Not is this offer fair. They already accepted that. The question is what happens to them next week, when the house is sold and they still have to live somewhere.

When no one answers that question, worry fills the silence. Give them a clear answer, and confidence fills it instead.

The strategy

One woman. One voice. Saying the thing nobody else says.

Not a spokesmodel and not a logo with a phone number. Someone who feels like the neighbor you'd call before making a decision this big.

She becomes the voice across the website and social. She explains how the offer is actually calculated. She walks through what 7 to 30 days really looks like, day by day. She names, out loud, everything you cover along the way.

The shift is simple. Less about the transaction, more about what happens after it.

The first 30 days

What happens, week by week.

Week 1

Position and cast

We build the messaging around what you cover, and map the seller types, because a woman settling her mother's estate and a landlord who is finished with tenants need to hear different things. In parallel, we source candidates for the face of the brand and run test reads.

Week 2

Produce

One batched production day. The website welcome video plus a full month of short-form, shot at a real property. Scripts are written before anyone is on camera, so the day produces a month of material instead of a handful of clips.

Week 3

Launch

Profiles rebuilt around the face. Daily posting begins on Facebook and Instagram. Welcome video goes live on the site. Your acquisition team gets the objection library, organized by what a seller is likely to say, so they know what to send and when.

Week 4

Read and adjust

What held attention, what people asked in comments, which questions repeated. Month two gets written from that, not from preference. In this category the comments are research, and they're free.
